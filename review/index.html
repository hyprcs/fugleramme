Introduction

Hi Arne, I'm building a Fugleramme in Sydney and have prepared twenty Australian bird additions for Classic, including familiar ones such as the magpie, Sulphur-crested Cockatoo, galah, noisy miner, Common Myna and white ibis. They're cut from original historical illustrations, mainly Gould's *The Birds of Australia*, with a Keulemans myna from Legge's *A History of the Birds of Ceylon*. I've retained the artists' credits and individual source links, checked the current species names, and used ordinary cropping, masking and resizing. There are no AI-generated or repainted birds in the contribution. I did use Codex to help with research, preparation tools and checks. The files have been prepared with your artwork tool and reviewed in individual previews and full collages, including alongside existing Classic images. Source-specific notes are included, such as the darker Sri Lankan form in the myna illustration. I haven't tested them on a physical panel yet. Would a PR adding these twenty to Classic be useful? I've put together a review pack with previews and source records, and can split the contribution if a smaller first set would be easier to review. Thanks, Will
